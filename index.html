Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to some common questions about our plastering services.

Which areas do you cover?

Fenland Plastering is based in the Peterborough area and covers Peterborough, Cambridgeshire and surrounding areas. If you're unsure whether we cover your location, please get in touch.

What types of plastering work do you undertake?

We offer a range of plastering services for both domestic and commercial customers. Please contact us to discuss your project and requirements.

Do you provide free quotations?

Yes. We provide free, no-obligation quotations. Get in touch and tell us a little about your project.

Do you undertake domestic and commercial work?

Yes. We undertake both domestic and commercial plastering projects, working with homeowners and businesses.

How long does plastering work usually take?

Timescales depend on the size and nature of the job. We'll be happy to discuss the expected timescale when preparing your quotation.

Do I need to prepare the room before you arrive?

We'll discuss any preparation needed before work begins, so you know what to expect.

How do I arrange a quotation?

Simply get in touch using the contact details on our website. Tell us about the work you need and we'll arrange a free, no-obligation quotation.

Need a plasterer?

Get in touch with Fenland Plastering to discuss your requirements and request a quotation.
