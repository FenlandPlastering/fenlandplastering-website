Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Fenland Plastering respects your privacy and is committed to handling personal information responsibly. This policy explains what information may be collected when you visit this website and how it is used.

1. Who we are

This website is operated by Fenland Plastering, providing plastering services in Peterborough, Cambridgeshire and surrounding areas.

Fenland Plastering is responsible for personal information collected directly through its business activities.

2. Information we collect

The website is currently an information-only website. Its enquiry form is not active and does not submit or collect personal information.

If you contact the business by telephone or email once those contact details are published, we may receive personal information such as your name, contact details and information about your plastering enquiry.

We may also receive basic technical information when you visit the website, such as your IP address and information about your browser or device, as described below.

3. How we use your information

Information you provide directly to the business may be used to:

  • Respond to enquiries and requests for quotations.
  • Discuss and arrange plastering work.
  • Communicate with customers about their projects.
  • Meet any applicable legal obligations.

4. Website hosting and third-party services

This website is currently hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub may collect technical information about visitors, including IP addresses, for security purposes.

The website also currently loads a background photograph from Unsplash. When that image is requested, your browser connects to Unsplash, which may receive technical information such as your IP address and browser details.

These services operate under their own privacy policies. You can read them here:

  • GitHub Privacy Statement
  • Unsplash Privacy Policy

5. Cookies and analytics

We do not currently use Google Analytics or intentionally install advertising or analytics tracking tools on this website.

The website does not currently use a cookie-based advertising or analytics system. However, third-party services used to deliver website content may use their own technologies. Please refer to their privacy policies for further information.

6. How long we keep information

If you contact the business, information will only be kept for as long as reasonably necessary to deal with your enquiry, provide services, and meet any applicable legal or accounting requirements.

7. Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be shared where necessary to provide our services, use relevant service providers, or comply with legal obligations.

8. Your privacy rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may have rights under UK data protection law to request access to your personal information, ask for corrections or deletion, object to certain uses, or request that processing is restricted.

You can also raise concerns with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO): ico.org.uk .
